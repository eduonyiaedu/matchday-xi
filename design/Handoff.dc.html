05 · Club accent, end to end

Accent reaches: active tab + underline, empty pitch slots, filled jerseys, card hairline on the next-fixture card, focus rings, avatar, your pinned leaderboard row, shirt numbers in the squad drawer. It does not tint page backgrounds — the pitch green stays constant so a Newcastle fan's app isn't unreadable.

09 · Crests & IP

Club crests appear on onboarding tiles, leaderboard rows and share cards. Your own code comments already avoid kit artwork deliberately, so every crest slot has a designed fallback: a solid disc in the club's primary colour with the three-letter code in Oswald. Ship the fallback if crest licensing isn't settled — the layouts are built around the disc, not the crest, so nothing shifts. The mockups show the fallback throughout for that reason.

10 · Icon & PWA

The mark is a gold shield on a near-black field: #050B08 tile, gold shield interior, chalk centre circle and halfway line at 42%, and the XI in pitch-green sitting centred on the halfway line. The splash stacks the Matchday XI wordmark above the shield in the same chalk/gold pairing used in the app header, with the oversized YOU beneath it.

Export the shield mark at 192 / 512 / 512-maskable into public/icons/, replacing the current files. Maskable needs the shield scaled to 80% inside the safe zone — the gold field bleeds to the edge and takes the crop. Update themeColor in layout.tsx from #0f172a to #0B1F17 — it's currently slate, which flashes blue-grey on launch before the app paints.
