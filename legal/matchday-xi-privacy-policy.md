# Matchday XI — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** September 23, 2026

## 1. Who We Are
Matchday XI ("we," "us") operates the Matchday XI football prediction platform at https://matchday-xi-eduonyiaedu-7577.vercel.app/. For privacy-related questions, contact: eduonyiaedu@gmail.com.

## 2. What Data We Collect
- **Account information:** email address, display name, password (stored securely, never in plain text).
- **Usage data:** your selected favorite team, submitted predictions, points earned, login dates (for streak tracking), and private competition memberships/creations.
- **Technical data:** IP address, device/browser information, collected for security and service-reliability purposes.

We do not currently collect payment or financial information, as paid features are not yet active.

## 3. How We Use Your Data
We use your data to:
- Operate your account, predictions, and leaderboard standings.
- Calculate login streaks and prize eligibility.
- Communicate service updates, including matchday reminders.
- Maintain security and prevent abuse (e.g. duplicate-account detection).
- Improve the Service over time.

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing
We process your personal data on the basis of your consent (given at account creation) and as necessary to perform our contract with you (i.e. providing the Service you signed up for), consistent with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

## 5. Third Parties We Share Data With
- **Hosting and database infrastructure providers** (used to run and store the Service) — these providers process account and usage data on our behalf as data processors, under their own security and confidentiality obligations.
- **Sports data providers** (used to retrieve fixture schedules and official lineups) — these providers do not receive your personal account information; data flows one-way from them into the Service, not the reverse.
- **Bot-protection provider** (Cloudflare Turnstile, on the sign-up and log-in forms) — checks that a real person, not an automated script, is creating or signing in to an account. It processes technical data from your browser for that check only.
We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

## 6. Data Retention
We retain your account and usage data for as long as your account remains active, and for a reasonable period afterward as needed for legal, security, or record-keeping purposes. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 8).

## 7. Cookies & Similar Technologies
The Service uses a small number of first-party cookies necessary for login and core functionality. One of these holds a random device identifier (it contains nothing about you or your device) so we can recognise when several accounts are used from the same device — used only for fair play in prize draws and prizes (duplicate-account detection), never for advertising, and never shared. Accounts found this way are not blocked; they may be excluded from prizes, subject to review. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

## 8. Your Rights
Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, you have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you.
- Request correction of inaccurate data.
- Request deletion of your account and associated data.
- Object to certain processing of your data.
- Withdraw consent at any time (which may limit your ability to use the Service).

To exercise these rights, contact us at eduonyiaedu@gmail.com.

## 9. Children's Privacy

## 10. Data Security

## 11. International Data Transfers
Our hosting and database infrastructure may store or process data outside Nigeria. Where this occurs, we take steps consistent with NDPA requirements to ensure an adequate level of protection for transferred data.

## 12. Changes to This Policy
